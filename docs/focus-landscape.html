FOCUS Ecosystem Landscape

The FOCUS Ecosystem

The FinOps Open Cost and Usage Specification (FOCUS) normalizes billing datasets across AI, cloud, SaaS, data center, and other technology vendors. This landscape maps the data generators who produce FOCUS data and the consumers who put it to work.
